SHOT 1.0 is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/coin-or/SHOT/releases/tag/releases%2F1.0.1). Binaries are available for Windows, and on Linux and macOS SHOT can be compiled with the [compilation instructions](/getting-started/#source).

SHOT is also available in GAMS 31.1. A trial version can be [downloaded from GAMS](https://www.gams.com/download/).

As of version 1.0, SHOT can also solve nonconvex MINLP problems as a heuristic, with valid bounds on the objective value. Some nonconvex problem types are solved to global optimality through automatic reformulations.
