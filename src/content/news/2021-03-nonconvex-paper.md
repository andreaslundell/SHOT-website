The full paper about the nonconvex features in SHOT is available online, open access, in the Journal of Global Optimization. It also includes benchmarks on nonconvex MINLP and MIQCQP problems.

Lundell, A. and Kronqvist, J. *Polyhedral approximation strategies for nonconvex mixed-integer nonlinear programming in SHOT*. Journal of Global Optimization 82(4), 863–896 (2022). [doi:10.1007/s10898-021-01006-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10898-021-01006-1)
