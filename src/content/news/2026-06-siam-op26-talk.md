At the SIAM Conference on Optimization (OP26) in Edinburgh, Andreas Lundell presented *What's new in SHOT 2.0*. The talk covered:

- HiGHS as a MIP subsolver and its performance on the convex MINLP instances in MINLPLib,
- the new Python interface SHOTpy,
- the extended callbacks, and what they make possible: warm starts, custom primal heuristics, external cut generation and lazy MINLP constraints,
- the decomposition of nonseparable convex quadratic expressions, the convex bounding strategy for nonconvex problems and parallel primal heuristics,
- the roadmap after version 2.0: a JuMP interface, SCIP as a MIP subsolver, presolve and more reformulations of nonconvex terms.
