---
title: Call SHOT through the GAMS links for Pyomo and JuMP
date: 2020-07-01
type: news
summary: With GAMS installed, SHOT can be called from Pyomo and JuMP through the GAMS interfaces.
link: https://www.gams.com/blog/2020/06/new-and-improved-gams-links-for-pyomo-and-jump/
---

If you have a licensed version of GAMS (you can also request a [free community license](https://www.gams.com/latest/docs/UG_License.html)), you can call SHOT through the GAMS interfaces for Pyomo and JuMP. Read more in the [GAMS blog post](https://www.gams.com/blog/2020/06/new-and-improved-gams-links-for-pyomo-and-jump/), in the [GAMS.jl](https://github.com/GAMS-dev/gams.jl) repository, or in the documentation of the [Pyomo interface](https://pyomo.readthedocs.io/en/stable/).
