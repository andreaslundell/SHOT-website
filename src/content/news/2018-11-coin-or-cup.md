Andreas Lundell, Jan Kronqvist and Tapio Westerlund won the [2018 COIN-OR Cup](https://www.coin-or.org/2018-coin-or-cup-winner/) for their work on the Supporting Hyperplane Optimization Toolkit, a new COIN-OR solver for MINLP.
