A new preprint describes the nonconvex features in SHOT, and includes benchmarks on nonconvex MINLP and MIQCQP problems:

Lundell, A. and Kronqvist, J. *Polyhedral approximation strategies in nonconvex mixed-integer nonlinear programming*. Optimization Online (2020). [optimization-online.org](http://www.optimization-online.org/DB_HTML/2020/03/7691.html)

The final version was published in the [Journal of Global Optimization](/news/2021-03-nonconvex-paper/).
