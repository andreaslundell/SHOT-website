title: SHOT available on the NEOS Server
date: 2020-08-25
type: news
summary: Submit problems in GAMS format to SHOT on the NEOS Server, without installing anything.
link: https://neos-server.org/neos/solvers/minco:SHOT/GAMS.html
---

SHOT is available as a solver on the NEOS Server. You can [submit jobs in GAMS format](https://neos-server.org/neos/solvers/minco:SHOT/GAMS.html).

The [NEOS Server](https://neos-server.org/neos/) (Network-Enabled Optimization System) is a free internet-based service for solving numerical optimization problems.
